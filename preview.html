Dia Browser Guide: Features, AI Tools, and What Happens to Arc

A practical guide to Dia Browser by The Browser Company. Learn how its multi-tab AI reasoning, Morning Brief, and Arc migration wizard work.

by Dev Mimrot   November 13, 2026

The Browser Company has officially unveiled Dia, their next-generation AI-first web browser designed to fundamentally rethink how we interact with information online. While Arc revolutionized tab management with spaces, pinned tabs, and vertical sidebars, Dia pivots toward proactive machine intelligence.

Multi-Tab AI Reasoning

Unlike traditional browser extensions that summarize an individual active page, Dia can synthesize intelligence across 10 or 20 background tabs at once. If you are researching flight tickets, hotel options, or technical API docs, Dia creates a unified synthesis table without manual copying.

"Dia is not just a browser with a chatbot taped onto the sidebar. It is built from the ground up so the AI understands your ongoing tasks."

The Morning Brief & Migration From Arc

For millions of existing Arc users, the pressing question is continuity. The Browser Company provides an automated Migration Wizard that ports your Spaces, Pinned Tabs, and Profiles seamlessly into Dia.

  • Spaces to Channels: Your favorite spaces convert into reactive channels.
  • Morning Brief: A quick digest summarizing newly updated tabs while you slept.
  • Keyboard shortcuts: Fully compatible with Arc's command palette.
